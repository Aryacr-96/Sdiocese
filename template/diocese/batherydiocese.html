Suthan Bathery Diocese

The diocese of Sultan Bathery lies extended in Wayanad and Kannur district of the State of Kerala & the Nilgiris of the Tamil Nadu State. Geographically, the Diocese is blessed with different levels of elevation,the hills touching the skies and being skirted round with thick rain-fed sylvan forests rendering a specific charm that fire one’s wanderlust. The Diocese Headquaters - Nirmalagiri Aramana is situated at Sulthan Bathery, (formerly known as Sultan's Battery) a town and municipality in Wayanad district of Kerala, India,having borders with Tamil Nadu and Karnataka states.The Diocese is headed by His Grace Dr. Geevarghese Mar Barnabas Metropolitan from 2022.
