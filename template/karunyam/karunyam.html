ABOUT KARUNYA SPARSHAM

Touching Lives Through Compassion

Karunya Sparsham is a humanitarian outreach program dedicated to supporting individuals and families facing medical, financial, and social hardships. Inspired by the values of love, mercy, and service, the initiative seeks to bring hope and dignity to those in need.

Through healthcare assistance, awareness programs, palliative care support, and partnerships with hospitals and charitable organizations, Karunya Sparsham continues to make a meaningful impact in the lives of many.
